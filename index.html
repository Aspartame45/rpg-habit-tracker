✦ WELCOME, ADVENTURER!

RPG Task Tracker turns your daily habits and to-dos into an RPG character sheet. Complete real-life tasks to earn points in 9 attributes (Strength, Focus, Intelligence, Charisma, Creativity, Initiative, Discipline, Agility, Vitality), level up, unlock class titles, and earn cosmetics for your pixel-art character.

Getting started:

  • Check off tasks on the DAILY tab to earn attribute points and build streaks.
  • Add your own custom tasks from PROJECTS / task management.
  • Visit the CHARACTER tab to see your sprite, class title, and total level.
  • Your mood reflects how recently you've worked on each active attribute — keep them all warm!
  • Unlock and equip cosmetics (hats, weapons, capes) as you level up your stats.

⚠ Your data is stored only in this browser (localStorage). It is not synced or backed up anywhere. Use the EXPORT button regularly to save a backup file, and IMPORT to restore it.
