✦ WELCOME, ADVENTURER!

RPG Task Tracker turns your daily habits and to-dos into an RPG character sheet. Complete real-life tasks to earn points in 9 stats (Strength, Focus, Intelligence, Charisma, Creativity, Initiative, Discipline, Agility, Vitality), and keep your sprite in good spirits.

Getting started:

  • Check off tasks on the DAILY tab to earn stat points and build streaks.
  • Add your own custom tasks from PROJECTS / task management.
  • Your mood reflects how recently you've worked on each active stat — keep them all warm!

⚠ Your data is stored only in this browser (localStorage). It is not synced or backed up anywhere. Use the EXPORT button regularly to save a backup file, and IMPORT to restore it.
